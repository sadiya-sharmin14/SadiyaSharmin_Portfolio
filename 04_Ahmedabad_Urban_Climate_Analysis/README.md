
# Ahmedabad Urban Climate Analysis

## Project overview

A GIS and remote-sensing-based assessment of urban expansion and environmental change across the Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA) region, Gujarat, India, from 2015 to 2025.

## Research objectives

- Assess spatial patterns of urban expansion.
- Examine vegetation and land-surface temperature patterns.
- Analyse rainfall, terrain, and drainage characteristics relevant to flood susceptibility.
- Develop a preliminary flood-susceptibility assessment to inform climate-resilient planning.

## Study area

The study focuses on the AUDA region, covering approximately 1,866 km². It is distinct from the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) area.

## Selected findings

- Built-up area increased from 405.77 km² in 2015 to 460.34 km² in 2025.
- The increase was 54.57 km², or approximately 13.45%.
- NDVI, land-surface temperature, rainfall, drainage, and flood-susceptibility layers were examined.

## Methods and tools

- GIS and spatial analysis
- Satellite remote sensing
- Google Earth Engine and Python
- QGIS / ArcGIS Pro, as applicable to the final workflow

## Project materials

The report, selected map outputs, and reproducible analysis files will be added to this repository.

## Limitations

Flood susceptibility is a preliminary analytical assessment, not a validated flood forecast. Results should be interpreted alongside data limitations and further validation.
